- **Outer Loop (`i`):** The outermost loop can be parallelized because each iteration of this loop (`i`) operates on a different row of matrix `C` and is independent of other iterations.
- **Middle Loop (`j`):** The middle loop can also be parallelized because each iteration of `j` is independent within a given `i`.
- **Inner Loop (`k`):** The inner loop must remain sequential to maintain the correct order of computations, as modifying `C[i][j]` in parallel with different `k` values could lead to race conditions.
- **Collapse Clauses:** The `collapse(2)` clause is used to allow the middle and inner loops to be collapsed into a single parallel region, reducing overhead. This is safe because the parallel regions for different values of `i` do not overlap.
